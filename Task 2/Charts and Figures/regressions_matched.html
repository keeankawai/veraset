Linear probability models; dependent variable = 1 if the storefront is matched (an approved Veraset label in some open month of 2019-2021), 0 if not. Storefronts with no positive sales in any open month of 2019-2021 are dropped. Opening year: year of the first month with positive retail sales (baseline 2014). Opening duration: months open in 2019-2021 (baseline <= 12). Sales: mean monthly net sales over open months, 2019-2021. Place density: 2020 population per square mile of land in the storefront's census place; storefronts outside any place drop out of the columns that use it. Heteroskedasticity-robust (HC1) standard errors in parentheses. * p < 0.1, ** p < 0.05, *** p < 0.01.
